Why We Declare and Decree

Declarations of Faith & Truth

We align our words with God because Scripture teaches that life and death are in the power of the tongue (Proverbs 18:21), that we believe and confess (Romans 10:9–10), and that God's Word accomplishes what He sends it to do (Isaiah 55:11).

Declarations

Proclaim what is true — aligning our words with the reality of God's Word and who He says we are.

Decrees

Enforce what God has already established — releasing His authority through the name of Jesus. (Luke 10:19)

The People Behind the Mission

About the Authors

Pastors Joanne and Ryan Lee are the founding pastors of Blessed International, a global apostolic movement marked by prayer, revival, and a passion to see the nations transformed by the power of God. While faithfully serving in a Korean-American church in Anaheim, California, their lives were radically interrupted by a profound visitation of the Holy Spirit. In that defining moment, God called them into His purposes for an end-time move that would extend beyond local ministry and touch nations around the world.

In simple obedience, they began gathering with a small group in their living room to seek the Lord in prayer. On August 15, 2004, Blessed International was birthed in that humble setting. As of 2026, Blessed International includes over 200 churches and equipping centers across 16 nations, with its headquarters in Santa Ana, California.

Together, Pastors Joanne and Ryan continue to lead Blessed International with a singular vision: to see lives awakened, the Church strengthened and prepared, and the nations reached through the transforming power of the Holy Spirit.

Co-Lead Pastor · Blessed International

Pastor Ryan Lee

Pastor Ryan practiced law for ten years before responding fully to the call of ministry. He later earned his Master of Divinity from Talbot Theological Seminary and was ordained as a pastor. His teaching carries both theological depth and practical clarity, equipping believers to walk out their calling with conviction and purpose.
